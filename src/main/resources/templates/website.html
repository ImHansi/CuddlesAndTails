Services

---------------------------------

Micro Chipping

Microchipping is a safe and effective way to provide permanent identification for pets, increasing the likelihood of their return if they become lost.

Blood Pressure Monitoring

A reliable way to measure BP and vital signs that is fast, simple and silent. Utilizing an animal-specific BP algorithm with our motion-tolerant technology.

Consultation

A consultation for your pet involves a vet assessing your animal's health, discussing any concerns, and providing recommendations for care.This may include a physical exam, vaccinations, parasite control, and nutritional advice.

Vaccination

Vaccinations are crucial for protecting pets from preventable, life-threatening diseases. For dogs, core vaccines typically include protection against canine distemper, adenovirus (hepatitis), parvovirus, and rabies.

Surgery Facility

These facilities provide a range of surgical services, including routine procedures like spaying and neutering, as well as more complex operations for injuries, illnesses, or other conditions.

X-Ray Facility

By utilizing the advanced imaging capabilities of X-rays, veterinary professionals can provide comprehensive care and accurate diagnoses for your beloved pets.
